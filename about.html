About One Dundas關於壹登

Serviced apartments in the heart of Kowloon.座落九龍核心的服務式公寓。

Nestled in Mongkok佇立旺角

Located at the heart of Kowloon, One Dundas Serviced Apartment is set on the residential side of bustling Mongkok and yet just a step away from a colourful, lively neighbourhood surrounded by traditional shops, local businesses and conveniently connected by public transportation.壹登座落於九龍的核心地帶，佇立在繁華旺角的寧靜住宅區中；然而，只要輕輕跨出一步，就能立刻擁抱那座色彩繽紛、充滿煙火氣的熱鬧街區。傳統老舖與在地小店錯落其間，隨時準備好讓你探索最道地的香港風情，加上四通八達的便捷交通，讓你輕鬆穿梭城市每個角落。

With 109 well-appointed Studio, One-Bedroom and Two-Bedroom apartments, One Dundas suits singles, overseas students and small families alike. Expansive windows capture city or Victoria Harbour views, while kitchenettes come equipped with cookware and utensils.壹登精心打造了109間設施齊全的開放式、單卧室及雙卧室公寓，無論單身、海外留學生還是小家庭都非常合適。大片落地玻璃窗盡覽城市或維港景色，廚房備齊烹調器具與餐具。
